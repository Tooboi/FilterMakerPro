# Filter Maker Pro 0.6.6

Shared hair and dust for every filter, hair that keeps the same width at any length, and a border that no longer lets the pleats poke through.

## ✨ What's new

- **One hair and one dust collection for the whole file.** Every filter uses the same **FM Hair Strands** and **FM Dust Pieces** collections inside *Filter Maker Library*, instead of each filter adding its own copies. Old per-filter collections are cleaned up automatically.
- **Hair with a constant width.** Strands keep exactly the **Width** you set along their whole length, whatever their length, and close to a point at both ends.
- **Clear shared vs. per-filter settings.** Curl, Strand Shapes, Dust Types, Piece Variants and the dust material are shared by all filters (marked in the panel). Density, length, width, lift, tilt, seeds and the hair material stay per filter.
- **Seeds only move placement.** A filter's hair/dust Seed changes where pieces land, not their shapes. Applying a preset sets the shared strand and dust look.

## 🛠 Fixed

- **Pleats poking through the border:**
  - The frame now reaches past the edge far enough to cover shell thickness and wear.
  - **Chamfer** and **Round** bevels are limited so they never uncover the pleat ends (raise Front/Back Extend or Overhang for a bigger bevel).
  - The **Lip** profile's rim now rises above the frame instead of stepping the inner part down.
  - Frames keep their full height where they bend around box corners and folds.
  - The frame's inner edge no longer folds back on itself at corners when the outline is densely cut (gaps and clipping at the corners).
- **Curved panels** (e.g. a section of a cylinder) now get their full border width.

## 📦 Install

**Requires Blender 4.2 LTS or newer** (tested on 5.2 LTS).

1. Download **`FilterMakerPro-0.6.6.zip`** below. Don't unzip it.
2. Drag it into Blender, or go to *Edit → Preferences → Get Extensions → ⌄ → Install from Disk…*
   (Legacy: *Add-ons → Install from Disk…*, then enable **Object: Filter Maker Pro**.)
3. Press <kbd>N</kbd> in the 3D Viewport and open the **Filter Maker** tab.

**Updating from 0.6.5:** remove the old version and restart Blender before installing. Existing filters pick up the shared collections and the new border the next time they update; press the refresh button in the panel header to rebuild one right away.

## ✅ Tested

45 / 45 headless tests pass in Blender 5.2, including:
- Border coverage on flat panels (all four profiles), a box wrap, an L fold and a curved cylinder section.
- The shared collections.
- Constant hair width.
