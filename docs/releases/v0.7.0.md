# Filter Maker Pro 0.7.0

The biggest update yet: ready-made filter shapes, a die-cut board face with wire mesh and printed labels, built-in materials with dirt, real UVs, and one-click game-ready bakes.

## ✨ What's new

**Shapes**
- **Add Filter Shape:** Panel, Round Panel, Cartridge, V-Bank, Box Wrap and **Die-Cut Furnace**, each one click at the 3D cursor.
- **End caps** for cartridges and box wraps, with an optional centre hole.

**Face, Wire & Print** (new panel)
- **Die-cut face:** a board sheet over the front, back or both sides with **Diamond** or **Square** openings. You set openings across, opening shape, strut width, corner cut and board thickness. It works on flat, curved, folded and ring-shaped filters.
- **Wire mesh** pleated together with the media, diagonal or square.
- **Pull tabs** on the long sides.
- **Printed graphics:** a size label that works out the nominal size for you (for example "16 x 25 x 1"), airflow text and an arrow. Print on the sides, the front or both; the front strip reads upright and can carry its own text, like a brand or "MERV 8".

**Materials and UVs**
- **Built-in materials:** Paper, Synthetic, HEPA, Fibreglass and Carbon media, plus Painted, Galvanised, Aluminium, Cardboard, White Board, Plastic and Rubber frames. All procedural, no image textures.
- **Dirt:** one slider dirties the middle and the folds and keeps the frame edge clean. It's also available as the `fm_dirt` and `fm_edge` mesh attributes for your own shaders.
- **UVs on everything:** the pleated sheet is unrolled across the folds, so textures keep their size on every flank, frame and cap. Choose **Real-World Size** or **Fit 0-1**.

**Game-ready**
- One click builds a low-poly copy (low-poly pleats or a flat card), packs its UVs, and bakes Normal / AO / Base Color / Roughness from the full filter, including hair, dust, wire and print. It can export FBX or glTF.
- The die-cut face is baked into the maps, so the 16 x 25 x 1 furnace filter becomes a **132-triangle** card.
- Bakes use your GPU when one is enabled in *Preferences → System*, with only a few samples for the noise-free maps.
- Results go into a "*filter* Low" collection.

## 🔄 Changed

- **Piece variants are per filter.** The shared hair and dust collections hold as many pieces as the filter that asks for the most, and each filter picks from the first N. Curl, dust types and the dust material stay shared.
- New filters start with the Paper media and Painted frame; the built-in presets set matching materials. There's a new **Die-Cut Furnace** preset.
- Faster mesh assembly for face and curve filters.
- Works with Blender 5.x shader node names (texture outputs renamed from `Fac` to `Factor`).

## 📦 Install

**Requires Blender 4.2 LTS or newer** (tested on 5.2 LTS).

1. Download **`FilterMakerPro-0.7.0.zip`** below. Don't unzip it.
2. Drag it into Blender, or go to *Edit → Preferences → Get Extensions → ⌄ → Install from Disk…*
   (Legacy: *Add-ons → Install from Disk…*, then enable **Object: Filter Maker Pro**.)
3. Press <kbd>N</kbd> in the 3D Viewport and open the **Filter Maker** tab.

**Updating from 0.6.x:** remove the old version and restart Blender before installing. Existing filters keep their settings; they pick up UVs the next time they update (press the refresh button in the panel header to rebuild one right away). Materials stay as they were until you pick a built-in Media or Frame.

**Free edition:** [Filter Maker Lite 0.7.0](https://github.com/Tooboi/FilterMakerLite) now includes the Boolean tool. Filters made in Lite open in Pro unchanged.
